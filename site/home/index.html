Built for India

Following India's health and data rules.

Urban Health is designed around Indian law and guidance. Where a rule is still being phased in, we say so openly, and we work only with partners who hold the licences their work requires.

DPDP

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025

Clear notices, purpose-bound consent, the right to access, correct and erase your data, a nominee, and a Grievance Officer. We are aligning with each phase as it comes into force.

Aligning with the phased roll-out
TPG

Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020

Online consultations only with doctors registered with the National Medical Commission or a State Medical Council, with consent and a record of each consult.

Designed to follow
IT

Information Technology Act, 2000 and SPDI Rules, 2011

Reasonable security practices for sensitive personal data, including health records, with a published privacy policy and a way to raise concerns.

Designed to follow
ABDM

Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABHA)

Built to work with ABHA health IDs and consent-based record sharing. Integration is being prepared and will be offered only once approved.

Integration in preparation
Rx

Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Rules

Medicines come only from partner pharmacies with a valid drug licence. Prescription medicines need a valid prescription, checked by a registered pharmacist.

Licensed partners only
LAB

Diagnostic labs and doctors

Certificates and registration numbers are checked by two different members of our team before a partner joins, and expiry dates are watched daily.

Verified by two people

Privacy policy, in short

The fine print, made plain.

A short summary of how we handle your information. The full policy always governs if anything here differs.

What we collect
Only what you give us, or allow us to use:
  • Account details: name, mobile number, email.
  • Health details you add: reports, medicines, allergies, visits and family profiles.
  • Location, only when you book a home visit or use SOS, and only with your permission.
  • Basic technical logs that keep the service secure.
In simple words: we keep what you put in, nothing hidden.
Why we use it
To show your health information, explain reports, send reminders, arrange the tests, visits and medicines you ask for, help in an emergency, and keep accounts safe, only for the purposes you agreed to.
In simple words: only to help you, never to sell to you.
Who we share it with
Only the people you choose, for the task you choose: the lab you book, the doctor you consult, the pharmacy you order from, or family and contacts you add. Service providers who host our systems in India act on our instructions. We share with authorities only when the law requires it.
In simple words: only the people helping you get to see it.
How long we keep it
While your account is active, or as long as the law requires for medical and financial records. When you delete your account, personal data is erased after a short grace period, except what we must keep by law.
In simple words: when you leave, your data leaves too.
Your rights
  • See a summary of your data and how it is used.
  • Correct or update anything that is wrong.
  • Withdraw consent and erase your data.
  • Nominate someone to act for you if you cannot.
  • Raise a grievance, and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India if it is not resolved.
In simple words: it's your data, so you're the boss of it.
Children's data
Profiles for children under 18 are created and managed by a parent or lawful guardian, with their consent. We do not track children's behaviour or show them advertising.
In simple words: grown-ups look after kids' profiles.
Grievance Officer
For any question or concern about your data, write to our Grievance Officer at support@urbanhealth.in with the subject "Grievance". We acknowledge every request and keep you updated until it is resolved.
In simple words: if something feels wrong, tell us and we'll fix it.

Questions

Asked by families like yours.

Is Urban Health free to use?
Creating your account and adding your family is free. Lab tests, consultations and medicines you choose to book are paid to the provider, and the price is always shown before you confirm.
In simple words: Starting is free. You see every price before you pay.
Does it replace my doctor?
No. Urban Health helps you understand and organise your health information. Diagnosis and treatment always come from a registered doctor.
In simple words: It explains. Your doctor decides.
Where is my data stored?
Health records are stored in India, in Google Cloud data centres in Mumbai. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.
In simple words: Your data stays in India.
Can I look after my parents and children?
Yes. Add profiles for the people you care for. Parents can use a simple mode with larger text, children's profiles are managed by a parent or guardian, and you decide who can see what.
In simple words: One app for the whole family.
Which languages are available?
English, Telugu and Tamil today, with more Indian languages on the way.
In simple words: Three languages now, more coming.
How do I delete my data?
You can delete your account from Settings at any time. Personal data is erased after a short grace period, except what the law requires us to keep.
In simple words: You can leave whenever you like, and your data leaves with you.
What happens when I press SOS?
Holding SOS for one second alerts your emergency contacts with your location and Medical ID, and helps you call 112. If you can, always call 112 directly in an emergency.
In simple words: Hold for a second, and your people know.
Urban Health helps you understand and organise health information. It does not replace a doctor's advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call 112, or 108 for an ambulance.
