Questions.

What’s in the complete bag?

14 essentials. For you, Mee Mee nursing bras and breast pads, with Pee Safe maternity pads, disposable underwear and intimate wash. For your baby, SuperBottoms jhablas and newborn sets, with Mee Mee diapers, wipes, swaddles, towels and a padded carry nest. It all comes in a Mee Mee diaper backpack, with a free Urban Health emergency card.

Are these the brands’ own products?

Yes. Every item comes sealed in its maker’s own pack, with the maker’s name, address and MRP printed on it, and we never charge more than the MRP.

What should I bring from home?

Your MCP card, reports, ID and insurance card, two or three front-open nighties, clothes to go home in, slippers, toiletries, a water bottle, snacks and your phone charger. Many hospitals provide a gown and slippers, so it’s worth asking yours. The packing checklist keeps this list with your account.

When will it arrive?

We suggest a day before week 36, worked out from your due date. You can pick an earlier or later day at checkout.

How can I pay?

Pay when the bag arrives, by cash or UPI. Your receipt appears in the Urban Health app.

Can I return something?
  • Cancel free of charge until your bag is packed.
  • Return or exchange unused items in their original packing within 7 days of delivery. A size that doesn’t fit is exchanged once, free.
  • For hygiene reasons, maternity pads, disposable underwear, nursing bras, breast pads, intimate wash and wipes can’t be returned once opened, unless they arrive damaged or wrong.
  • Damaged or wrong item? Tell us within 48 hours of delivery with a photo, and we’ll replace it or refund it.
  • Refunds go back the way you paid, or to your UPI ID if you paid on delivery, usually within 5–7 working days of the item reaching us.
