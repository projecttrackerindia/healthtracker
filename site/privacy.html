In short

Urban Health helps you keep your family's health information in one place. We built it so that your health records stay on your own device and leave it only when you choose to share them.

  • Without an account, your health card, family profiles, reports, results, medicines, readings, pregnancy logs and Medical ID stay in your browser on your device.
  • When you sign in, your family's profiles, results, medicines, readings, cards and settings are encrypted and stored on our servers in India, so each family member you invite sees the same records on their own phone. Photos and PDFs stay on the device where they were added.
  • Our servers also keep what is needed to run your account: your name, email, mobile number, sign-in details, plan and payments, and orders when ordering is available in your area.
  • A report or prescription is read by our servers only when you tap Analyse and agree. It is processed for that one reading and not stored by us.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use your health information for marketing.

This policy explains how we handle personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

On your device and in your family's encrypted store

The app saves these in your browser's private storage on the device you use, and, once you sign in, in your family's encrypted store on our servers (AES-256-GCM, keys held separately from the data, India region):

  • Health card: name, date of birth, sex, blood group and mobile number for you and each family member you add.
  • Reports, prescriptions, scans and photos you upload to the Vault, and the results read from them.
  • Medicines, dose history, readings (heart rate, blood pressure, sugar, weight), visits, emergency contacts, Medical ID, consent history and pregnancy logs.

Photos and PDFs are not copied to other devices or to our servers; clearing your browser's site data removes them. Use Family & Privacy → My data to download a copy or delete everything, which also deletes the family's store on our servers when you are its owner.

Family members you invite can see the shared records. A profile switched to Private in Consent is hidden from other accounts.

Reading reports with AI

When you tap Analyse on a document and agree, the document and the details needed to explain it (first name, age, sex, pregnancy status, allergies, conditions, current medicines, height and weight) are sent securely to our server and to our AI processing partner for that one reading.

  • The result comes back to your device. We do not keep the document or the result on our servers; we keep only a count of readings for your plan.
  • Our AI processing partner acts only on our instructions. Under its commercial terms it does not use this data to train its models and deletes it after a short period kept for abuse monitoring.
  • You can switch AI reading off at any time in Family & Privacy → Consent.

AI reading explains results in plain language. It is not a diagnosis. Always discuss results with your doctor.

Emergencies (SOS)

SOS opens your phone's own dialer for 112 or 108 and prepares a message to your emergency contacts with your location and Medical ID. The message is sent from your phone, by you. Your location is read only when you press SOS or tap Use my location.

Your rights and choices

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • See the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Correct or update it. Most details can be changed in the app.
  • Delete it. Delete on-device data in Family & Privacy → My data, and your account in Settings → Account & security → Delete account.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example for AI reading or sharing with a doctor. Withdrawal does not affect what was done before.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you are unable to.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Children and family members

You must be 18 or older to create an account. A parent or legal guardian may add children and manage their records, and by doing so confirms they have the authority to do so. We do not knowingly track children or use their data for advertising. Add an adult family member's details only with their permission.

How we protect data

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and browsers are told to always use it.
  • Passkeys let you sign in with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, so there is no password to steal.
  • Passwords must be strong and are checked against known data breaches before they are accepted; we store only a one-way hash.
  • Repeated wrong attempts lock sign-in for a while, and you can sign out of all devices at any time.
  • We limit access to production systems to the people who need it to run the service.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change applies. The date at the top shows the latest version.
