Questions

Good to know

What is a passkey, and why should I use one?

A passkey replaces your password with the fingerprint, face or screen lock you already use on your phone or computer. It can't be guessed, reused or phished, and it syncs through iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager. You can still keep a password and one-time codes as backups.

Where are my health records stored?

Without an account, everything stays in your browser on your device. When you sign in, your family's profiles, results, medicines and cards are encrypted and stored on our servers in India, so each family member sees them on their own phone. Photos and PDFs stay on the device where they were added. A document you analyse with AI is sent for that one reading and not kept.

Does Urban Health diagnose illness?

No. It explains results in plain language and suggests questions and checks to discuss with your doctor. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional care. In an emergency call 112 or 108.

What makes a password acceptable here?

At least 12 characters with upper- and lower-case letters, a number and a symbol; no three identical characters in a row; no runs like 1234 or qwer; nothing from your name, email or phone; and nothing that appears in common-password lists or known data breaches. The form checks each rule as you type, or can suggest a strong one.

Can my whole family use one account?

Yes. One account holds profiles for everyone you care for, each with their own records, medicines and consent settings. Up to 12 people, and it is free for everyone until 31 March 2027.

Can I see my watch data here?

Yes, through the phone app: Android reads Health Connect (steps, sleep, heart rate, weight, blood pressure, sugar, SpO₂) and iPhone reads Apple Health including Apple Watch ECG recordings. Fitbit links directly from the web. No watch maker lets third-party apps stream a live ECG from the watch itself; for a live ECG in the browser use a Polar H10 or similar chest strap.

Is it really free? What happens in April 2027?

Yes — every feature, for your whole family, with no card and nothing to cancel, until 31 March 2027. We earn a small share from the labs and pharmacies you order through, never from you and never from your records; your health data is not sold to anyone. Before anything changes we will tell you well in advance, and the records you have added stay yours either way.

Can I order medicines or book lab tests?

Ordering and lab booking are opening city by city. Every pharmacy and lab is checked by our team, with its licence or NABL number, before it appears. Prices come from their own lists and are confirmed when you order or book.
