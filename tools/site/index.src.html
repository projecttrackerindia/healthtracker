Security & privacy

Health data deserves better than a password.

Sign in with a passkey — your fingerprint, face or screen lock — so there's nothing to steal or phish. Passwords, when you use them, must meet strict rules and are checked against known breaches.

Passkeys (FIDO2)

Phishing-resistant sign-in that works with your phone's fingerprint, face or PIN and syncs across your devices.

One-time codes

Six-digit codes by email or SMS for new devices, recovery and optional two-step sign-in. Ten-minute expiry, five tries.

Strong passwords only

Questions

Good to know

What is a passkey, and why should I use one?

A passkey replaces your password with the fingerprint, face or screen lock you already use on your phone or computer. It can't be guessed, reused or phished, and it syncs through iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager. You can still keep a password and one-time codes as backups.

Where are my health records stored?

Reports, photos, results and your Medical ID are stored in your browser on your device. Our server stores your account, orders, plan and ratings. When you choose to analyse a document with AI, it is sent for that one analysis and not kept.

Does TheHealthTracker diagnose illness?

No. It explains results in plain language and suggests questions and checks to discuss with your doctor. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional care. In an emergency call 112 or 108.

What makes a password acceptable here?

At least 12 characters with upper- and lower-case letters, a number and a symbol; no three identical characters in a row; no runs like 1234 or qwer; nothing from your name, email or phone; and nothing that appears in common-password lists or known data breaches. The form checks each rule as you type, or can suggest a strong one.

Can my whole family use one account?

Yes. One account holds profiles for everyone you care for, each with their own records, medicines and consent settings. The Free plan covers 5 people, Family 8 and Plus 12.

Which pharmacies and labs are listed?

Partners are verified by our team before they appear, with their licence or NABL number. Prices come from their own price lists and are confirmed when you order or book.
